    About

    Diktátor is a small offline-capable web app for practising Czech dictation. Install it from your browser menu (“Add to Home Screen”).

    Spelling and numbers are played from recorded clips bundled with the app, so they work on any device. Words and sentences need a voice: either your phone’s own Czech text-to-speech voice (on Android install Czech in Google Text-to-speech settings; on iOS add a Czech voice under Settings › Accessibility › Spoken Content › Voices), or the built-in voice chosen in Settings, which downloads about 95 MB once and then runs inside the browser, offline.

    Data and acknowledgements

    The word and sentence packs are derived works released under CC BY-SA 4.0; the app code is MIT. They are built from:

    • NTU-MC Czech WordNet (CC BY 4.0), Bond et al. 2013, based on Czech WordNet 2.1 from the NLP Centre, Masaryk University (Rambousek, Horák & Pala 2018).
    • English Wiktionary (CC BY-SA 4.0) by its contributors, extracted with Wiktextract (Ylonen 2022): glosses, IPA, recording names.
    • Open Multilingual Wordnet English (WordNet 3.0 licence), Bond & Foster 2013, based on Princeton WordNet 3.0 (Fellbaum 1998).
    • FrequencyWords by Hermit Dave (CC BY-SA 4.0), from OpenSubtitles2018 (Lison & Tiedemann 2016): frequency bands.
    • Tatoeba (CC BY 2.0 FR): sentences and translations; each result links to its Tatoeba page and contributors.
    • Wikimedia Commons and Lingua Libre recordings (CC BY-SA, per file): streamed on demand; each result links to the file page with its author and licence.
    • Piper text-to-speech (MIT) by Michael Hansen and the Rhasspy project, with the Czech voices jirka (dataset CC0) and kasandra (recordings by Ondřej Šimek, CC BY 4.0) from rhasspy/piper-voices. The bundled letter and number clips were synthesised with jirka. The built-in voice runs in the browser with ONNX Runtime Web (MIT) and piper-phonemize / espeak-ng (GPL-3.0) compiled to WebAssembly by Diffusion Studio.

    Source code and full references: github.com/bond-lab/diktator.
