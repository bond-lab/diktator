# Licence of the data packs

`words.json` and `sentences.json` are derived works. Because they include
content under share-alike licences (Wiktionary, FrequencyWords), the packs as a
whole are released under **CC BY-SA 4.0**
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). The upstream sources and
their licences are:

| Source | Used for | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| NTU-MC Czech WordNet, Bond et al. (2013) | word pool, parts of speech | CC BY 4.0 |
| English Wiktionary, extracted with Wiktextract (Ylonen 2022) via kaikki.org | glosses, IPA, audio file names | CC BY-SA 4.0 (text also available under GFDL) |
| Open Multilingual Wordnet, English (omw-en 2.0), Bond & Foster (2013); Princeton WordNet 3.0 | glosses for words not in Wiktionary | WordNet 3.0 licence |
| FrequencyWords by Hermit Dave, from OpenSubtitles2018 (Lison & Tiedemann 2016) | frequency ranks | CC BY-SA 4.0 |
| Tatoeba | sentences and English translations; each carries its Tatoeba id | CC BY 2.0 FR |

Human recordings are not included in the packs. The app streams them on demand
from Wikimedia Commons and links to each file's page, where its own licence and
author are shown (Lingua Libre recordings are CC BY-SA 4.0; others vary).

See the project README for full references.
